Privacy Policy

ReelPrep, ReelPost and ReelStudio Last updated 18 August 2026

This policy describes how these tools handle data. They are programs that run on the computer of the person who installs them. That person, referred to here as the operator, is the one who decides what gets published and to which Facebook Pages.

What the software stores

Access tokens issued by Facebook, along with the folders, posting times and captions the operator configures. All of it is written to files inside the program's own folder, on the operator's computer.

Where data is sent

Videos and captions chosen by the operator are sent to Facebook's official Graph API so that they can be published to Pages the operator administers. Facebook is the only destination.

There is no server belonging to the author of this software. Nothing is uploaded to one, because none exists. There is no analytics service, no advertising network and no third party of any kind in the path.

What is collected from other people

Nothing. The software does not read, collect or store personal data belonging to visitors, followers, or anyone who interacts with a Page.

Retention and deletion

Because everything is held locally, the operator controls all of it. Data is removed by deleting the program's folder. Access can be withdrawn at any time from Facebook Settings, under Apps and Websites, which invalidates the stored tokens immediately.

Who to contact

Each installation is operated independently. For anything concerning a particular Facebook Page, including its content or a request about your own data, contact the owner of that Page; they operate their own copy of the software and hold their own data.
